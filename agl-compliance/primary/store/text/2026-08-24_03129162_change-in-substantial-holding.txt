Notice of change in substantial holder notice  
We act for the Galipea Partnership (ABN 43 843 920 211) (Galipea Partnership). On behalf of Galipea 
Partnership, we attach a Form 604 (Notice of change of interests of substantial holder) in respect of AGL 
Energy Limited (AGL).  
On 20 June 2023, Galipea Partnership announced it: 
1. held a relevant interest in 70,037,429 fully paid ordinary shares in AGL ( Shares) representing 10.41% 
of AGL Shares on issue; 
2. continued to hold to cash settled put and call options in relation to 6,859,875 reference Shares 1 (Initial 
Collar); and 
3. had entered into further cash settled put and call options in respect of 13,000,000 reference Shares 
(Subsequent Collar). 
We are instructed as follows: 
4. Galipea Partnership and its affiliates have taken further steps to simplify their interest in AGL.  
5. The Initial Collar was subsequently amended and reduced to reference 6,727,472 Shares ( Amended 
Collar). 
6. Galipea Partnership has now terminated the Amended Collar in full respect of all 6,727,472 reference 
Shares and disposed of an equal number of AGL Shares.  
7. Galipea Partnership now holds a relevant interest in 63,309,957 Shares representing 9.41% of Shares 
on issue.  It continues to hold the Subsequent Collar on terms previously disclosed to the ASX.
